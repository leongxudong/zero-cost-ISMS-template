# Business Impact Analysis Template

A Business Impact Analysis (BIA) identifies critical business processes, impact tolerance, dependencies, and recovery priorities.

## BIA Table

| Process | Owner | Impact if Unavailable | Maximum Tolerable Downtime | Target Recovery Time | Data Loss Tolerance | Dependencies | Workaround |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|  |  | Operational / Financial / Regulatory / Reputational |  |  |  | Systems / People / Vendors / Facilities |  |

## Impact Categories

| Category | Example Impact |
|---|---|
| Operational | Service delivery disruption |
| Financial | Revenue loss, additional cost, penalty, or funding impact |
| Regulatory | Breach of contractual, regulatory, or reporting obligation |
| Reputational | Loss of trust from stakeholders, clients, or partners |
| Safety | Harm to users, staff, or beneficiaries |

## BIA Questions

- What process is being assessed?
- Who owns the process?
- What systems are required?
- What data is required?
- What vendors or third parties are required?
- How long can the process be unavailable?
- What workaround exists?
- What is the minimum staffing required?
