# Internal Audit Checklist

A lightweight internal audit checklist for reviewing whether key ISMS components exist and are operating.

## Checklist

| Area | Question | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scope | Is the ISMS scope defined and approved? | Scope document | Not Reviewed / Adequate / Gap |
| Policy | Is the information security policy approved and communicated? | Policy, communication record | Not Reviewed / Adequate / Gap |
| Risk | Is there an active risk register? | Risk register | Not Reviewed / Adequate / Gap |
| Assets | Is there an asset inventory with owners? | Asset inventory | Not Reviewed / Adequate / Gap |
| Access | Are access reviews performed? | Review record | Not Reviewed / Adequate / Gap |
| Vendors | Are vendors assessed based on risk? | Vendor register, assessment record | Not Reviewed / Adequate / Gap |
| Incident | Is there an incident response procedure? | IR procedure, test record | Not Reviewed / Adequate / Gap |
| BIA | Are critical processes assessed? | BIA records | Not Reviewed / Adequate / Gap |
| BCP | Is there a continuity plan? | BCP document, test record | Not Reviewed / Adequate / Gap |
| DRP | Is IT recovery documented and tested? | DRP, restore test evidence | Not Reviewed / Adequate / Gap |
| Awareness | Is security awareness conducted? | Training record | Not Reviewed / Adequate / Gap |
| Improvement | Are corrective actions tracked to closure? | CAP tracker | Not Reviewed / Adequate / Gap |

## Finding Quality

A useful audit finding should include:

- Condition observed
- Criteria or expectation
- Risk or impact
- Evidence
- Owner
- Corrective action
- Due date
