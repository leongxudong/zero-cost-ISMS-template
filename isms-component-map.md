# ISMS Component Map

This page maps the minimum components of a functional ISMS and explains how they connect.

## Core Components

| ISMS Area | Artefact | Owner | Review Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Governance | ISMS scope statement | Management / InfoSec | Annual |
| Governance | Information security policy | Management / InfoSec | Annual |
| Risk | Risk register | InfoSec / Risk owners | Quarterly |
| Risk | Risk treatment plan | InfoSec / Risk owners | Quarterly |
| Assets | Asset inventory | IT / Asset owners | Monthly / Quarterly |
| Access | Access review record | IT / System owners | Quarterly |
| Vendors | Vendor risk register | Procurement / IT / InfoSec | Annual or upon renewal |
| Incident | Incident response plan | IT / InfoSec | Annual and after incidents |
| Resilience | Business Impact Analysis | Business owners | Annual |
| Resilience | Business Continuity Plan | Business owners | Annual |
| Resilience | Disaster Recovery Plan | IT | Annual or after major change |
| Assurance | Internal audit checklist | InfoSec / Internal audit | Annual |
| Improvement | Corrective action tracker | Control owners | Monthly |
| Leadership | Management review pack | Management / InfoSec | Annual / Semi-annual |

## Minimum Operating Rhythm

| Frequency | Activity |
|---|---|
| Monthly | Review open incidents, corrective actions, and urgent risks |
| Quarterly | Review risk register, access reviews, vendor changes, and key metrics |
| Semi-annually | Review policy exceptions and awareness status |
| Annually | Review ISMS scope, policies, BIA, BCP, DRP, audit results, and management review |

## Design Principle

A functional ISMS does not need expensive tooling at the start. It needs clear scope, ownership, risk visibility, evidence, review cadence, and continual improvement.
