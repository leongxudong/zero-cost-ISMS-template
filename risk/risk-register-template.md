# Risk Register Template

A lightweight risk register for a zero-cost ISMS.

## Risk Register

| Risk ID | Risk Statement | Asset / Process | Impact | Likelihood | Risk Rating | Existing Controls | Treatment Plan | Owner | Due Date | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-001 | If [event] occurs due to [cause], then [impact] may result. |  | High / Medium / Low | High / Medium / Low | High / Medium / Low |  | Accept / Mitigate / Transfer / Avoid |  |  | Open / In Progress / Closed |

## Risk Statement Format

Use this structure:

```text
If [threat/event] occurs due to [vulnerability/cause], then [business/security impact] may result.
```

## Treatment Options

| Option | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Accept | Business accepts the residual risk |
| Mitigate | Implement controls to reduce likelihood or impact |
| Transfer | Shift part of the risk through contract, insurance, or outsourcing |
| Avoid | Stop the activity or remove the source of risk |

## Review Cadence

- Review high risks monthly.
- Review all risks quarterly.
- Update risks after incidents, audit findings, major changes, or vendor changes.
